Research

  • 2025/05 - 2025/07
    Stellar Evolution Atlas

    Independent research project computing a grid of ~2000 stellar evolution models using MESA across stellar masses from 0–100 M☉ and varying metallicities. Analyzed HR diagram evolution, stellar lifetimes, and temperature–density tracks. Results presented through an interactive Stellar Evolution Atlas website with an open dataset of stellar models.
