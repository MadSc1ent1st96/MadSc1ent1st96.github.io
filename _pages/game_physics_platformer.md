## How this works

### Physics Concepts in Action

**Gravity Zones**
- **Normal Zone (Blue)**: Standard Earth-like gravity
- **Low Gravity (Purple)**: Reduced gravity for higher jumps
- **High Gravity (Red)**: Increased gravity for challenging jumps
- **Bouncy Zone (Orange)**: Automatic bounce when landing
- **Energy Zone (Green)**: Recharges energy when on ground

### Core Physics Mechanics

**Energy Management**
- Limited jumps that consume energy
- Energy recharges in specific zones
- Strategic energy conservation required

**Momentum Conservation**
- Player maintains velocity between zones
- Realistic physics with friction and air resistance
- Terminal velocity prevents infinite acceleration

**Gravity Effects**
- Different gravitational forces in each zone
- Affects jump height and fall speed
- Demonstrates gravitational acceleration

## Physics Learning Goals

### **Motion Physics**
- **Kinematics**: Position, velocity, and acceleration relationships
- **Dynamics**: How forces affect motion
- **Energy Conservation**: Managing kinetic and potential energy
- **Momentum**: Conservation principles in motion

### **Gravitational Physics**
- **Gravitational Acceleration**: Different g values in zones
- **Free Fall**: How objects move under gravity
- **Terminal Velocity**: Maximum fall speed
- **Bouncing**: Elastic collisions with surfaces

### **Energy Physics**
- **Energy Transfer**: Converting between energy types
- **Energy Conservation**: Total energy remains constant
- **Power**: Rate of energy consumption
- **Efficiency**: Maximizing energy usage
