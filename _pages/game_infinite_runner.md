## Controls

- **Desktop**: Press SPACEBAR, Arrow Up, or W to jump
- **Mobile**: Tap the "🕹️ Jump" button or anywhere on screen
- **Touch Devices**: Tap anywhere on the game area

---

## How to Play

- **Jump**: Use double jump to avoid obstacles
- **Collect**: Green power-ups give bonus points
- **Survive**: The longer you last, the higher your score
- **Speed**: Game gets faster as you progress

Enjoy the game!
