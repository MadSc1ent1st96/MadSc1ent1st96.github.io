## Features

### **🎮 Gameplay**
- **Smooth Controls**: Responsive jumping mechanics
- **Pattern-Based Obstacles**: Fair and predictable challenges
- **Power-up Collection**: Collect green power-ups for bonus points
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Speed increases as you progress
- **Particle Effects**: Visual feedback for actions

### **🎯 Controls**
- **Desktop**: Press SPACEBAR, Arrow Up, or W to jump
- **Mobile**: Tap the "🕹️ Jump" button or anywhere on screen
- **Touch Devices**: Tap anywhere on the game area

### **🏆 Scoring**
- **Base Score**: Points increase as you survive longer
- **Power-up Bonus**: +100 points for each power-up collected
- **High Score**: Try to beat your personal best!

## Game Mechanics

### **Jump System**
- **Double Jump**: Up to 2 jumps before landing
- **Ground Reset**: Jumps reset when touching the ground
- **Smooth Physics**: Realistic gravity and movement

### **Obstacle Patterns**
- **Single Obstacles**: Basic challenges
- **Double Obstacles**: Two obstacles with gaps
- **Low Obstacles**: Wide but short barriers
- **High Obstacles**: Tall but narrow barriers

### **Power-ups**
- **Green Power-ups**: Collect for bonus points
- **Visual Effects**: Particle effects when collected
- **Strategic Placement**: Appear at regular intervals

## Tips for High Scores

1. **Master the Double Jump**: Use both jumps strategically
2. **Watch for Patterns**: Obstacles follow predictable patterns
3. **Collect Power-ups**: Don't miss the green bonus items
4. **Stay Focused**: Speed increases over time
5. **Practice**: The more you play, the better you'll get!
