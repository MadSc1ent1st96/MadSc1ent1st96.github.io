# Infinite Runner 🏃‍♂️
A classic platformer with a physics twist! Jump, dodge obstacles, and collect power-ups.

**Controls:**
- **Desktop**: Press SPACEBAR, Arrow Up, or W to jump
- **Mobile**: Tap the "🕹️ Tap to Jump" button or anywhere on the game area
- **Touch Devices**: Tap anywhere on the game area

## Game Features
- Double jump capability
- Power-up collection for invincibility
- Progressive difficulty
- High score tracking
- Sound effects and background music
