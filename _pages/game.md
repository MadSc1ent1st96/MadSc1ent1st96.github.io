## **🎯 Physics Learning Goals**

These games are designed to help you understand:
- **Wave Physics**: Interference, superposition, and wave properties
- **Motion Physics**: Velocity, acceleration, and collision detection
- **Interactive Learning**: Visual and hands-on physics concepts
- **Computational Thinking**: Real-time calculations and simulations

## **🔬 Educational Value**

### **Wave Interference Constructor**
- **Wave Superposition**: See how waves combine mathematically
- **Constructive Interference**: When waves add up to create larger amplitude
- **Destructive Interference**: When waves cancel each other out
- **Beat Frequency**: Observe beating patterns with different frequencies
- **Real Physics Equations**: Uses actual wave equations from physics

### **Infinite Runner**
- **Motion Physics**: Jump mechanics and gravity
- **Collision Detection**: Physics-based obstacle avoidance
- **Energy Conservation**: Power-up collection and scoring
- **Reaction Time**: Physics of quick decision making

## **🎓 Learning Resources**

### **Wave Physics Concepts:**
- **Wave Function**: y(x,t) = A sin(kx - ωt + φ)
- **Superposition Principle**: When waves meet, they add algebraically
- **Interference Patterns**: Result from wave combination
- **Standing Waves**: Special interference patterns

### **Motion Physics Concepts:**
- **Kinematics**: Position, velocity, acceleration relationships
- **Dynamics**: Forces and motion (gravity, collision forces)
- **Energy**: Kinetic and potential energy in motion
- **Momentum**: Conservation in collisions
