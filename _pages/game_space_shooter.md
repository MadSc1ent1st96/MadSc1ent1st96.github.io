## Controls

- **Desktop**: Move mouse to control ship, SPACEBAR to fire
- **Mobile**: Touch and drag to move ship, tap "🔥 Fire" button to shoot
- **Touch Devices**: Tap anywhere on screen to fire

---

## How to Play

- **Move**: Control your spaceship with mouse/touch
- **Shoot**: Fire at enemy ships to destroy them
- **Dodge**: Avoid asteroids and enemy collisions
- **Collect**: Green power-ups restore health
- **Survive**: Manage your health bar and get the highest score!

Enjoy the game!
